THIET KE CO SO DU LIEU - HE THONG DAT SAN CAU LONG

Huong thiet ke:
- DBMS de xuat: PostgreSQL.
- Extension nen dung: PostGIS, btree_gist, pgcrypto/citext neu can.
- Kieu khoa chinh: UUID.
- Thoi gian: timestamptz.
- Tien te: numeric(12,2).
- Vi tri ban do: geography(Point, 4326).
- Du lieu linh hoat nhu cau hinh thuat toan, payload webhook, metadata thong bao: JSONB.


1. NHOM TAI KHOAN, DANG NHAP, PHAN QUYEN

1.1. users
Muc dich: luu thong tin tai khoan chung cua moi nguoi dung.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- full_name varchar(150)
- email varchar(255) unique, nullable neu chi dang nhap bang phone
- phone varchar(20) unique, nullable
- password_hash text, nullable neu tai khoan tao bang Google
- avatar_url text
- email_verified_at timestamptz
- phone_verified_at timestamptz
- status varchar(30) -- active, inactive, banned, pending
- last_login_at timestamptz
- created_at timestamptz
- updated_at timestamptz

Index/constraint:
- unique lower(email) where email is not null
- unique phone where phone is not null

1.2. auth_providers
Muc dich: luu tai khoan dang nhap ben ngoai nhu Google OAuth.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- user_id uuid FK -> users.id
- provider_name varchar(50) -- google
- provider_user_id varchar(255) -- Google sub
- provider_email varchar(255)
- provider_email_verified boolean
- provider_avatar_url text
- access_token_encrypted text, nullable
- refresh_token_encrypted text, nullable
- token_expires_at timestamptz, nullable
- linked_at timestamptz
- last_used_at timestamptz
- created_at timestamptz
- updated_at timestamptz

Index/constraint:
- unique(provider_name, provider_user_id)
- unique(user_id, provider_name)
- index user_id

Ghi chu:
- Neu chi dung Google de dang nhap, khong bat buoc luu access_token/refresh_token.
- Neu co luu token thi phai ma hoa, khong luu plain text.

1.3. password_reset_requests
Muc dich: luu yeu cau xac minh va dat lai mat khau qua email hoac so dien thoai.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- user_id uuid FK -> users.id
- channel varchar(20) -- email, phone
- destination varchar(255) -- email hoac so dien thoai nhan ma
- verification_hash text -- chi luu hash cua OTP/token, khong luu ma goc
- expires_at timestamptz
- verified_at timestamptz, nullable
- used_at timestamptz, nullable
- attempt_count smallint default 0
- resend_count smallint default 0
- requested_ip inet, nullable
- user_agent text, nullable
- created_at timestamptz

Index/constraint:
- index(user_id, created_at desc)
- index(destination, created_at desc)
- index(expires_at) where used_at is null
- check channel in ('email', 'phone')
- check attempt_count >= 0
- check resend_count >= 0

Ghi chu:
- Moi OTP/token chi duoc su dung mot lan; danh dau used_at sau khi doi mat khau thanh cong.
- Nen gioi han so lan thu, so lan gui lai va tan suat yeu cau theo tai khoan/IP.
- Khong tra ve thong tin cho biet email/so dien thoai co ton tai hay khong.

1.12. email_verification_requests
Muc dich: ma OTP xac minh email luc dang ky tai khoan.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- user_id uuid FK -> users.id
- email varchar(255) -- dia chi da gui, nguoi dung co the doi email sau do
- verification_hash text -- chi luu hash cua OTP, khong bao gio luu ma goc
- expires_at timestamptz
- verified_at timestamptz, nullable -- chi dat khi day dung la ma xac minh thanh cong
- used_at timestamptz, nullable -- da dung xong hoac bi ma moi hon thay the
- attempt_count smallint
- resend_count smallint
- requested_ip inet
- user_agent text
- created_at timestamptz

Index:
- index(user_id, created_at desc)
- index(email, created_at desc)
- index(expires_at) where used_at is null

Ghi chu:
- Bang rieng chu khong dung chung password_reset_requests: hai luong co vong doi va
  y nghia khac nhau. Gop chung thi phai them cot purpose va moi cau truy van deu phai
  nho loc theo cot do, som muon se quen mot cho.
- Tai khoan moi tao o trang thai pending va khong dang nhap duoc. Nhap dung ma thi
  chuyen sang active, dat users.email_verified_at va cap token luon.
- Moi ma chi dung mot lan; gui ma moi thi danh dau cac ma cu la da dung.
