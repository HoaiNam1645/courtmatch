THIET KE CO SO DU LIEU - HE THONG DAT SAN CAU LONG

Huong thiet ke:
- DBMS de xuat: PostgreSQL.
- Extension nen dung: PostGIS, btree_gist, pgcrypto/citext neu can.
- Kieu khoa chinh: UUID.
- Thoi gian: timestamptz.
- Tien te: numeric(12,2).
- Vi tri ban do: geography(Point, 4326).
- Du lieu linh hoat nhu cau hinh thuat toan, bang chung/metadata va metadata thong bao: JSONB.

Mo hinh nghiep vu da chot:
- Courtly la nen tang ket noi va tinh hoa hong; Courtly khong nhan/giu tien san cua nguoi choi.
- Nguoi choi thanh toan truc tiep cho chu san bang tien mat hoac chuyen khoan.
- Chu san xac nhan da thu tien; booking chi confirmed sau buoc xac nhan nay.
- Sau booking hoan thanh, he thong tinh hoa hong va ghi cong no chu san voi Courtly.
- Cong no duoc doi soat/thanh toan theo ky; khong con luong rut tien va chi tra tu Courtly cho chu san.
- Pham vi hien tai chi co mot chu san. Tai khoan OWNER do admin tao, khong co dang ky chu san cong khai.


1. NHOM TAI KHOAN, DANG NHAP, PHAN QUYEN

1.1. users
Muc dich: luu thong tin tai khoan chung cua moi nguoi dung.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- full_name varchar(150)
- email varchar(255) unique, nullable neu chi dang nhap bang phone
- phone varchar(20) unique, nullable
- password_hash text, nullable neu tai khoan tao bang Google
- avatar_url text
- email_verified_at timestamptz
- phone_verified_at timestamptz
- status varchar(30) -- active, inactive, banned, pending
- last_login_at timestamptz
- created_at timestamptz
- updated_at timestamptz

Index/constraint:
- unique lower(email) where email is not null
- unique phone where phone is not null

1.2. auth_providers
Muc dich: luu tai khoan dang nhap ben ngoai nhu Google OAuth.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- user_id uuid FK -> users.id
- provider_name varchar(50) -- google
- provider_user_id varchar(255) -- Google sub
- provider_email varchar(255)
- provider_email_verified boolean
- provider_avatar_url text
- access_token_encrypted text, nullable
- refresh_token_encrypted text, nullable
- token_expires_at timestamptz, nullable
- linked_at timestamptz
- last_used_at timestamptz
- created_at timestamptz
- updated_at timestamptz

Index/constraint:
- unique(provider_name, provider_user_id)
- unique(user_id, provider_name)
- index user_id

Ghi chu:
- Neu chi dung Google de dang nhap, khong bat buoc luu access_token/refresh_token.
- Neu co luu token thi phai ma hoa, khong luu plain text.

1.3. password_reset_requests
Muc dich: luu yeu cau xac minh va dat lai mat khau qua email hoac so dien thoai.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- user_id uuid FK -> users.id
- channel varchar(20) -- email, phone
- destination varchar(255) -- email hoac so dien thoai nhan ma
- verification_hash text -- chi luu hash cua OTP/token, khong luu ma goc
- expires_at timestamptz
- verified_at timestamptz, nullable
- used_at timestamptz, nullable
- attempt_count smallint default 0
- resend_count smallint default 0
- requested_ip inet, nullable
- user_agent text, nullable
- created_at timestamptz

Index/constraint:
- index(user_id, created_at desc)
- index(destination, created_at desc)
- index(expires_at) where used_at is null
- check channel in ('email', 'phone')
- check attempt_count >= 0
- check resend_count >= 0

Ghi chu:
- Moi OTP/token chi duoc su dung mot lan; danh dau used_at sau khi doi mat khau thanh cong.
- Nen gioi han so lan thu, so lan gui lai va tan suat yeu cau theo tai khoan/IP.
- Khong tra ve thong tin cho biet email/so dien thoai co ton tai hay khong.

1.4. roles
Muc dich: dinh nghia vai tro.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- code varchar(50) unique -- player, owner, staff, admin
- name varchar(100)
- description text
- created_at timestamptz

Cot chinh:

1.7. user_roles
Muc dich: mot user co the co nhieu vai tro.

Cot chinh:

Constraint:

1.9. court_owner_profiles
Muc dich: ho so cua chu san duy nhat trong he thong.

Cot chinh:

- bank_account_name varchar(150)

1.10. player_availability_slots

- end_time time

- check end_time > start_time

1.11. player_preferred_locations
Muc dich: luu khu vuc uu tien choi cua nguoi choi.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- user_id uuid FK -> users.id

- longitude numeric(10,7)

1.12. email_verification_requests
Muc dich: ma OTP xac minh email luc dang ky tai khoan.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- user_id uuid FK -> users.id
- email varchar(255) -- dia chi da gui, nguoi dung co the doi email sau do
- verification_hash text -- chi luu hash cua OTP, khong bao gio luu ma goc
- expires_at timestamptz
- verified_at timestamptz, nullable -- chi dat khi day dung la ma xac minh thanh cong
- used_at timestamptz, nullable -- da dung xong hoac bi ma moi hon thay the
- attempt_count smallint
- resend_count smallint
- requested_ip inet
- user_agent text
- created_at timestamptz

Index:
- index(user_id, created_at desc)
- index(email, created_at desc)
- index(expires_at) where used_at is null

Ghi chu:
- Bang rieng chu khong dung chung password_reset_requests: hai luong co vong doi va
  y nghia khac nhau. Gop chung thi phai them cot purpose va moi cau truy van deu phai
  nho loc theo cot do, som muon se quen mot cho.
- Tai khoan moi tao o trang thai pending va khong dang nhap duoc. Nhap dung ma thi
  chuyen sang active, dat users.email_verified_at va cap token luon.
- Moi ma chi dung mot lan; gui ma moi thi danh dau cac ma cu la da dung.

3. NHOM DAT SAN VA DUYET BOOKING

3.1. bookings
Muc dich: don dat san va toan bo vong doi duyet cua owner.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- booking_code varchar(50) unique
- user_id uuid FK -> users.id
- venue_id uuid FK -> venues.id
- status varchar(40) -- pending_owner_confirmation, awaiting_payment, confirmed,
  rejected, cancelled, completed, expired, disputed, refunded
- total_amount numeric(12,2) -- gia da chot, khong tinh lai sau khi tao booking
- price_locked_at timestamptz
- note text
- owner_response_expires_at timestamptz
- owner_responded_at timestamptz
- owner_responded_by uuid FK -> users.id, nullable
- payment_expires_at timestamptz
- confirmed_at timestamptz -- owner/staff xac nhan da thu tien
- completed_at timestamptz -- chi dat sau khi gio choi ket thuc
- rejected_at timestamptz
- rejection_reason text
- cancelled_at timestamptz
- cancellation_reason text
- created_at timestamptz
- updated_at timestamptz

Index:
- index(user_id, created_at desc)
- index(venue_id, status, created_at desc)
- index(status)
- index(owner_response_expires_at) where status = 'pending_owner_confirmation'
- index(payment_expires_at) where status = 'awaiting_payment'

Quy tac trang thai:
- Tao don -> pending_owner_confirmation.
- Owner chap nhan -> awaiting_payment.
- Owner xac nhan da thu tien -> confirmed.
- Qua gio choi va khong bi huy/tranh chap -> completed.
- Owner tu choi -> rejected; booking_items duoc giai phong.
- Khong dung completed de bieu dien da thu tien.

3.2. booking_items
Muc dich: chi tiet tung san/tung khung gio trong don.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- booking_id uuid FK -> bookings.id
- court_id uuid FK -> courts.id
- start_time timestamptz
- end_time timestamptz
- price numeric(12,2) -- snapshot gia cua khung gio
- status varchar(30) -- pending, confirmed, cancelled, completed, expired
- created_at timestamptz

Constraint:
- check end_time > start_time
- Exclusion constraint chong trung lich:
  court_id WITH = va tstzrange(start_time, end_time, '[)') WITH &&
  ap dung khi status in ('pending', 'confirmed')

Ghi chu:
- item giu status pending trong ca hai buoc cho owner va cho thanh toan.
- item chi confirmed khi owner/staff xac nhan da thu tien.

Index:
- index(court_id, start_time, end_time)
- index(booking_id)

3.3. booking_status_history
Muc dich: lich su bat bien cua moi thay doi trang thai booking.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- booking_id uuid FK -> bookings.id
- old_status varchar(40)
- new_status varchar(40)
- reason text
- changed_by uuid FK -> users.id, nullable neu he thong tu dong
- actor_role varchar(30), nullable -- player, owner, staff, admin, system
- created_at timestamptz


4. NHOM THANH TOAN TRUC TIEP, HOAN TIEN, TRANH CHAP

4.1. payments
Muc dich: ghi nhan viec nguoi choi thanh toan truc tiep cho owner; Courtly khong giu tien.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- booking_id uuid FK -> bookings.id
- recipient_owner_id uuid FK -> users.id
- method varchar(30) -- cash, owner_bank_transfer
- amount numeric(12,2)
- status varchar(40) -- awaiting_payment, payer_reported, confirmed, rejected,
  expired, disputed, partially_refunded, refunded
- bank_name varchar(100), nullable -- snapshot tai khoan owner luc thanh toan
- bank_account_no varchar(50), nullable
- bank_account_name varchar(150), nullable
- reference_content varchar(255), nullable
- proof_url text, nullable
- payer_reported_at timestamptz, nullable
- confirmed_by uuid FK -> users.id, nullable -- owner/staff
- confirmed_at timestamptz, nullable
- rejected_by uuid FK -> users.id, nullable
- rejected_at timestamptz, nullable
- rejection_reason text, nullable
- expired_at timestamptz, nullable
- created_at timestamptz
- updated_at timestamptz

Index/constraint:
- index(booking_id, created_at desc)
- index(recipient_owner_id, status, created_at desc)
- index(status)
- check amount >= 0

Ghi chu:
- Khong con provider = sepay, provider_transaction_id hay webhook xac nhan tu dong.
- Viec nguoi choi bam "da thanh toan" chi chuyen sang payer_reported.
- Booking chi confirmed khi owner/staff thuc hien xac nhan thu tien.

4.2. payment_status_history
Muc dich: luu lich su xac nhan/tu choi thanh toan de doi soat va tranh chap.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- payment_id uuid FK -> payments.id
- old_status varchar(40)
- new_status varchar(40)
- reason text
- changed_by uuid FK -> users.id, nullable neu he thong
- created_at timestamptz

Index:
- index(payment_id, created_at)

4.3. refunds
Muc dich: theo doi viec owner hoan tien truc tiep cho nguoi choi.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- payment_id uuid FK -> payments.id
- booking_id uuid FK -> bookings.id
- dispute_id uuid FK -> booking_disputes.id, nullable
- amount numeric(12,2)
- reason text
- status varchar(40) -- requested, approved, awaiting_owner_refund,
  owner_reported_refunded, confirmed, rejected, failed
- requested_by uuid FK -> users.id
- approved_by uuid FK -> users.id, nullable -- admin
- owner_reported_at timestamptz, nullable
- proof_url text, nullable
- confirmed_by uuid FK -> users.id, nullable -- player/admin
- confirmed_at timestamptz, nullable
- created_at timestamptz
- updated_at timestamptz

Ghi chu:
- Courtly khong tu chuyen tien hoan. Owner hoan truc tiep va gui bang chung.
- Refund confirmed se dieu chinh truc tiep commission_amount/status cua booking_commissions.

4.4. booking_disputes
Muc dich: tranh chap lien quan booking, thanh toan, dich vu va yeu cau hoan tien.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- booking_id uuid FK -> bookings.id
- payment_id uuid FK -> payments.id, nullable
- reporter_id uuid FK -> users.id
- category varchar(50) -- venue_closed, not_as_described, owner_cancelled,
  extra_charge, paid_not_confirmed, other
- description text
- reporter_evidences_json jsonb, nullable -- danh sach file/anh bang chung cua nguoi bao cao
- refund_requested boolean
- requested_refund_amount numeric(12,2), nullable
- status varchar(30) -- open, awaiting_owner_response, reviewing, resolved, rejected, cancelled
- owner_response_deadline timestamptz
- owner_response text, nullable
- owner_responded_by uuid FK -> users.id, nullable
- owner_responded_at timestamptz, nullable
- owner_evidences_json jsonb, nullable -- danh sach file/anh bang chung cua owner
- resolution varchar(30), nullable -- full_refund, partial_refund, no_refund, other
- resolution_note text, nullable
- resolution_evidences_json jsonb, nullable -- bang chung bo sung khi admin xu ly
- handled_by uuid FK -> users.id, nullable -- admin
- handled_at timestamptz, nullable
- created_at timestamptz
- updated_at timestamptz

Index:
- index(booking_id)
- index(status, created_at desc)
- index(owner_response_deadline) where status = 'awaiting_owner_response'

Ghi chu:
- Pham vi hien tai khong can luong chat tranh chap nhieu tin nhan.
- Noi dung bao cao, giai trinh owner va ket luan admin duoc luu truc tiep tren booking_disputes.
- Bang chung duoc luu dang mang JSONB gom file_url, file_type, description va uploaded_at;
  khong tao bang dispute_messages hoac dispute_evidences rieng.


5. NHOM HOA HONG, CONG NO, DOI SOAT DINH KY

5.1. platform_fee_configs
Muc dich: cau hinh chinh sach hoa hong cua Courtly.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- name varchar(150)
- fee_type varchar(30) -- percentage, fixed
- fee_value numeric(12,2)
- effective_from timestamptz
- effective_to timestamptz, nullable
- status varchar(30) -- active, inactive
- created_by uuid FK -> users.id
- created_at timestamptz

Ghi chu:
- Thay doi cau hinh khong duoc lam thay doi hoa hong cua booking cu.
- Moi booking_commissions phai snapshot fee_type va fee_value tai thoi diem ghi nhan.

5.2. booking_commissions
Muc dich: hoa hong Courtly cua tung booking da hoan thanh.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- booking_id uuid FK -> bookings.id unique
- owner_id uuid FK -> users.id
- fee_config_id uuid FK -> platform_fee_configs.id, nullable
- gross_amount numeric(12,2)
- fee_type varchar(30) -- snapshot percentage, fixed
- fee_value numeric(12,2) -- snapshot ty le/gia tri
- commission_amount numeric(12,2)
- settlement_id uuid FK -> commission_settlements.id, nullable
- status varchar(30) -- pending, due, suspended, adjusted, waived, settled
- calculated_at timestamptz
- due_at timestamptz
- settled_at timestamptz, nullable
- created_at timestamptz
- updated_at timestamptz

Index/constraint:
- unique(booking_id)
- index(owner_id, status, due_at)
- index(settlement_id)
- check gross_amount >= 0
- check commission_amount >= 0

Ghi chu:
- Chi tao mot lan khi booking completed; job/retry phai idempotent bang unique(booking_id).
- Khi booking disputed, commission chuyen suspended va tam loai khoi cong no den han.
- Refund confirmed tao adjusted/waived tuy ty le owner thuc su hoan.
- Cong no duoc tinh truc tiep tu cac booking_commissions den han, sau khi tru so tien da thanh
  toan cua commission_settlements; khong tao owner_commission_ledger.
- settlement_id gan hoa hong vao mot ky doi soat; mot booking commission chi thuoc toi da mot ky.

5.3. commission_settlements
Muc dich: ky doi soat/thanh toan hoa hong giua owner va Courtly.

Cot chinh:
- id uuid PK
- owner_id uuid FK -> users.id
- period_from date
- period_to date
- opening_outstanding numeric(12,2)
- commission_total numeric(12,2)
- adjustment_total numeric(12,2)
- amount_due numeric(12,2)
- amount_paid numeric(12,2)
- status varchar(30) -- open, pending_review, partial, paid, overdue, disputed
- owner_submitted_at timestamptz, nullable
- payment_reference varchar(255), nullable
- proof_url text, nullable
- reviewed_by uuid FK -> users.id, nullable -- admin
- reviewed_at timestamptz, nullable
- paid_at timestamptz, nullable
- note text
- created_at timestamptz
- updated_at timestamptz

Index/constraint:
- unique(owner_id, period_from, period_to)
- index(owner_id, status, period_to desc)
- check period_to >= period_from

Ghi chu tong quat nhom 5:
- Khong con withdrawal_requests va payout_transactions.
- Owner da nhan tien san truc tiep, nen Courtly khong co nghia vu chi tra/rut tien cho owner.
- Khong co owner_commission_ledger; chi tiet cong no doc truc tiep tu booking_commissions.
- Khong co commission_settlement_items; booking_commissions.settlement_id lien ket truc tiep den ky doi soat.

14. QUY TAC THIET KE QUAN TRONG

- Khong luu du lieu Google OAuth truc tiep vao users, ma luu trong auth_providers.
- password_hash trong users cho phep nullable de ho tro tai khoan Google-only.
- Khong tinh lai thong ke nguoi choi moi lan mo man hinh; doc tu player_statistics.
- Du lieu BWF duoc lam sach va chuyen doi bang script truoc khi ghi vao cac bang Courtly hien co; khong tao nhom bang BWF rieng trong database.
- Booking phai co rang buoc chong trung lich o database, khong chi check bang code.
- Public registration chi tao PLAYER. OWNER duy nhat do admin tao va cap role; khong co owner self-registration.
- Courtly khong giu tien san. Khong tao so du co the rut, withdrawal request hay payout cho owner.
- Nguoi choi bao da thanh toan khong du de confirmed booking; owner/staff phai xac nhan da thu tien.
- Booking completed chi sau khi gio choi ket thuc, tach biet voi payment confirmed.
- Gia booking va chinh sach hoa hong phai snapshot; thay doi bang gia/phi sau nay khong sua du lieu cu.
- Tinh hoa hong phai idempotent bang unique booking_id; khong ghi hai lan khi job retry.
- Cong no duoc tinh tu booking_commissions va so tien da ghi nhan tren commission_settlements;
  khong duy tri bang so cai cong no rieng.
- Moi booking_commissions lien ket truc tiep toi toi da mot commission_settlements bang settlement_id.
- Booking dang tranh chap chi tam hoan hoa hong cua booking do, khong khoa toan bo cong no owner.
- Refund do owner thuc hien truc tiep; Courtly theo doi bang chung, xac nhan va dieu chinh hoa hong.
- Map search nen dung PostGIS thay vi chi loc latitude/longitude thu cong.
- JSONB chi dung cho payload/cau hinh/metadata linh hoat; cac quan he chinh van nen chuan hoa bang FK.
